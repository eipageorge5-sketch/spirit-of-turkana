Some stories are not
told. They are
survived.

The Turkana people have endured drought, displacement, and a rapidly changing world — yet their culture, their wisdom, and their connection to the land remain unbroken. These are not stories of poverty. They are stories of resilience, identity, and a way of life that the modern world is only beginning to understand.

This archive exists to document them — in high definition, with care, and before the world moves on without looking.

Read the Stories

Document

High-definition photography and film capturing the people, landscapes, and traditions of Turkana County before they fade from the world's memory.

Advocate

Giving local communities a platform that reaches NGOs, conservationists, and global partners who have the resources to act on what they see.

Sustain

Building a self-sustaining archive through photography licensing, partnerships, and community support — so this work can continue indefinitely.
